The full-day format

FROM FIRST SHOTS,TO VISIBLE PROGRESS.

A private, structured shooting day designed around your level — starting with fundamentals and building toward a more confident, dynamic session.

01 / EXPERIENCE
01 · Start correctly

First, understand
what you are doing.

The day begins with clear guidance, handling fundamentals and a progression matched to your level. Beginners are welcome, and the pace is built around confidence rather than pressure.

02 · Build control

Progression you
can actually feel.

Professional instruction and individual teaching are central to the day. The objective is to develop confidence and technique through structured practice rather than simply accumulating shots.

03 · More dynamic

When your level

The experience can progress from first handling to more dynamic shooting, built step by step according to the participant’s level.

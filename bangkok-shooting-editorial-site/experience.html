The full-day format

從第一次握槍，到看得見的進步。

Bangkok Shooting Experience is presented as a private, structured shooting day designed around your level — starting with fundamentals and building toward a more confident, dynamic session.

01 / EXPERIENCE
01 · Start correctly

First, understand
what you are doing.

The experience is not positioned as an immediate rush to the trigger. The day begins with guidance, handling fundamentals and a progression matched to the participant’s level. Beginners are explicitly welcome.

02 · Build control

Progression you
can actually feel.

The public material emphasises professional instruction, individual teaching and visible progress. The objective is to develop confidence and technique through structured practice rather than simply accumulating shots.

03 · More dynamic

When your level
is ready, move.

The published experience description moves from first handling to dynamic shooting. This progression is presented as something built step by step according to the participant’s level.

Essential / Premium

Two ways to build
the same day.

The supplied Instagram material presents Essential as the core full-day private shooting experience and Premium as an all-in version adding the travel and content layer around it.

FAQ

Before you
send the DM.

Answers below stay within the details explicitly supported by the supplied Bangkok Shooting Experience content.

Is the experience suitable for beginners?
Yes. The supplied material explicitly describes the experience as suitable for beginners and says the progression is arranged step by step according to the participant’s level.
How many people can join?
The published overview states private sessions for 1 to 8 guests.
How much shooting is included?
The current overview in the supplied content states approximately 100 rounds per person. For the exact content of your chosen format and date, confirm directly with the team before booking.
Where does it take place?
The published location is Rangsit, north of Bangkok. A precise public street address is not included in the supplied material, so this site does not invent one.
Can transport from Bangkok be arranged?
Yes. The supplied content says Bangkok transfer can be arranged. Premium is also described as an all-in format that includes transport.
Can the day be photographed or filmed?
Yes. Professional photography and cinematic video are part of the published offer. The Premium description specifically includes professional photography, cinematic video and professional edited content.
How do I get the current price and available dates?
The official booking instruction is to DM your preferred date and number of participants. The supplied posts direct guests to Instagram for the complete package, price and availability details.

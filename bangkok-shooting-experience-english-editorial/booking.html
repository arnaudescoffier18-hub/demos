FAQ

Before you
send the DM.

Quick answers to the most useful questions before you request your date.

Is the experience suitable for beginners?
Yes. Yes. Beginners are welcome, and the progression is arranged step by step according to the participant’s level.
How many people can join?
Private sessions are designed for 1 to 8 guests.
How much shooting is included?
The experience overview states approximately 100 rounds per person. Confirm the exact content of your chosen format and date directly with the team before booking.
Where does it take place?
The experience takes place in Rangsit, north of Bangkok. Exact meeting and range details are confirmed during booking.
Can transport from Bangkok be arranged?
Yes. Yes. Bangkok transfer can be arranged, and the Premium format is designed as the more complete all-in option.
Can the day be photographed or filmed?
Yes. Yes. Professional photography and cinematic video are available, with the Premium format including the fuller content experience.
How do I get the current price and available dates?
DM your preferred date and number of participants to receive the current package, price and availability details.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Questions, answered.

Do I need previous shooting experience?+

No previous experience is required according to the experience description. Sessions are intended to progress from safety and fundamentals at a pace adapted to the participant.

Is this a quick tourist shooting session?+

No. The concept is positioned as a private, full-day experience rather than a short “choose a gun and fire a few rounds” visit. The focus is on understanding, progression and professional guidance.

How many people can join?+

The published format is for private groups from 1 to 8 participants. For larger groups or company activities, contact the team directly so they can confirm what is possible.

How many rounds are included?+

The published experience indicates approximately 100 rounds per participant. Exact package inclusions should be reconfirmed when booking because options can vary.

Can transport from Bangkok be arranged?+

Yes, Bangkok transfer arrangements are presented as an available option and are included in the Premium-style all-in experience described on the account. Confirm pickup details during booking.

Are photos and videos available?+

Professional photography and video are part of the premium positioning, including cinematic edited content designed to capture the day. Confirm the exact deliverables for your selected package.

Where exactly is the session?+

The public information places the experience in the Rangsit / north Bangkok area. Exact range and meeting logistics are best confirmed with the team for your date.

What should I send when I book?+

Send your preferred date and the number of participants. The account specifically invites guests to DM “BANGKOK” for current package details, pricing and available dates.

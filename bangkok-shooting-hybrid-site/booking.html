FAQ

Before you
book.

Useful answers based on the supplied and public experience information, without inventing fixed rules that should be confirmed for each booking.

Is the experience suitable for beginners?
Yes. The experience is presented as beginner-friendly and professionally supervised, with a progressive structure rather than assuming previous shooting experience.
How many people can join?
The published format is presented for private groups of 1 to 8 guests.
How many rounds are included?
The public overview describes approximately 100 rounds per participant. Confirm the exact current package details when booking.
Where does the experience take place?
The experience is presented in the Rangsit area, north of central Bangkok. Exact meeting and range details should be confirmed directly after booking.
Can transport be arranged?
Transfer arrangements are presented as an available option / part of broader formats. Confirm current pickup details and what is included for your package.
Is professional photo or video available?
Yes, the experience content presents professional photography and cinematic video as available options within broader packages.
How do I get the current price?
Send your preferred date and number of participants by Instagram DM or LINE. The team can then confirm the current rate and available format for that date.
